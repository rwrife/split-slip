# Split Slip support

- **A total does not match:** check the printed grand total, each line total, and any explicit fees or discounts. Every row needs chosen recipients before finalizing.
- **A rounding cent looks different:** whole cents use deterministic largest-remainder allocation. Review the extra-cent explanations in People; participant order resolves ties.
- **A finalized receipt needs correcting:** open it and choose **Duplicate to correct**. The original stays unchanged.
- **A photo cannot be imported:** choose a decodable image no larger than 12 MB and 48 million pixels. Working reference images are reduced to at most 4,096 pixels on their longest edge.
- **Move to another device:** save a private backup from **Your data**, transfer the complete folder, then restore it. Review the replacement confirmation first.
- **Restore failed:** the app attempts to restore its pre-operation copy. If recovery is pending, close and reopen the app; do not remove its sandbox or reinstall. Keep your exported backup.
- **Report a bug:** open an issue in [rwrife/split-slip](https://github.com/rwrife/split-slip/issues). Include app version, iOS version, and reproduction steps. Remove names, receipt photos, and other personal details from public reports.

The app supports Dynamic Type, system appearance, and labeled buttons. Photo zoom/pan has button alternatives. Real-device VoiceOver, Files-provider, and share-sheet validation is a release checklist item, not yet a certified accessibility claim.
