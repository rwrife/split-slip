Split shared meals and purchases with a clear total for each person. Split Slip lets you enter a receipt, choose who shares each item, and check the numbers before saving the result.

ENTER YOUR RECEIPT
Choose USD, EUR or GBP, enter the printed grand total, and add participant nicknames and item amounts. Record fee or discount adjustments explicitly.

CHOOSE HOW TO SPLIT
Assign items to selected people using equal shares or positive whole-number weights. Review unassigned rows and the difference between your entries and the receipt total.

REVIEW AND SAVE
Finalize only when every row is assigned and the totals match. Keep finalized receipts in local storage and review each person's share. Need a correction? Duplicate a finalized receipt into a linked draft while preserving the original.

Split Slip organizes the arithmetic. You enter and review the amounts; the app does not scan receipts or send payments.
