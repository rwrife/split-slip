Split shared meals and purchases with a clear total for each person. Split Slip helps you divide a receipt, review the amounts, and save or share the result.

START WITH JUST THE TOTAL
Enter the receipt total, add your people, and split equally. A starter item follows your total automatically, so you can finish without entering every item.

SPLIT YOUR WAY
Enter a fixed dollar amount for anyone and divide the remainder equally among the others. Automatic shares update when receipt amounts change. For detailed splits, enter individual items and choose who shares each one, with optional whole-number weights. Record fees and discounts explicitly. Supports USD, EUR, and GBP.

REVIEW EVERY SHARE
Check the totals before finalizing. Tap a person on a finalized receipt to expand their details. Item-based splits show their exact item amounts; whole-receipt splits show the shared items separately from their personal total. Duplicate a finalized receipt to make a correction while preserving the original.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR DATA
Search your saved receipts, preview text or CSV summaries, and share through the system share sheet. Export a private backup, restore a backup, or delete receipts and their local photos. Receipt data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it; device backups and Files providers follow your settings.

Split Slip organizes the arithmetic. You enter and review the amounts; the app does not scan receipts or send payments.
